The thread

My connection with my grandmother began with a thread, and this creative thread has changed shapes and forms throughout my life.

  1. 01

    A thread for the little hiccups

    In Brazil, when a baby has the hiccups, someone places a little piece of red thread on their forehead. My grandmother Zélia did it for me. That's where our thread begins.

  2. 02

    First stitches

    Later, she taught me the basics of hand sewing so I could sew for my Barbie dolls. The thread became a skill, passed from her hands to mine.

  3. 03

    A web of creative expressions

    Her lessons still guide my hands. Now I sew with tools of my own choosing, and every piece still starts with what she taught me.

It's Zélia's sewing thread. You'll find it on every label, every hang-tag, and on this site.

The handmade side

A collection by iaracreates

For the Love of Zelda is my handmade collection, named after my grandmother, Zélia.

I love maximalism: bold fabrics, big patterns, colour that makes you smile. Every bag mixes new fabrics with finds from thrift shops. I cut, piece and sew it by hand, and the scraps become patchwork for the next one, so as little as possible goes to waste.

No two bags are the same. I don't make products, I make pieces: bold, joyful, and made to bring a bit of colour and energy into your everyday.
