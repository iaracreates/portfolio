01Overview

What it is

A generative AI content design buddy that a fellow content designer and I built, starting at a hackathon, and rolled out to all product designers.

It’s grounded in our content strategy, guidelines, principles and word lists, and it recognises design system components. Designers can prompt it with text or images to get microcopy ideas with the reasoning behind them, and it tells them when it’s time to involve a content designer.

02The problem

Demand for content design kept growing

The same gap looked different from each side of the table.

For content designers

Small microcopy requests competed with strategic work, and we saw a chance to connect our guidelines and the design system.

For product teams

Many product designers worked without a dedicated content designer and wanted to write good copy on their own.

How might we

How might we scale content design, without people bypassing it where it matters?

03The journey

From hackathon to rollout

  1. 1

    Hackathon

    Two content designers prototyped the buddy, with instructions connected to our guidelines and the design system.

  2. 2

    Leadership demo

    A demo for product design leadership to collect feedback: what was working well, and what to adjust to fit the design team’s needs.

  3. 3

    Soft launch

    A pilot with a select group of designers. We collected their feedback and made small improvements.

  4. 4

    Full rollout

    The proof of concept was made available to all product designers.

04Setup, outcome and learnings

What we set up, and what we learned

Setup

  • Built for content designers and product designers
  • Covers the top 10 day-to-day use cases
  • Guidance on when to loop in and get help from a content designer when needed
  • Grounded in our guidelines, with the reasoning behind each suggestion

Outcome

  • Available to all product designers
  • Designed to free content designers from routine microcopy
  • Aimed at helping designers without dedicated support move faster

Learnings

  • Teams work independently from first sketches to pre-launch; content design keeps final approval
  • AI amplifies your guidelines, so sharpen the craft first
  • Design the hand-off, not just the answers
  • Stage it: demo, pilot, adjust, scale

The escalation rules were key: the buddy was designed to extend content design, not replace it.
