Blue Patchwork Tote Bag

Upcycled Fabrics

Thrifted fabricOne of a kind

A one-of-a-kind patchwork tote, cut and sewn by hand from thrifted and leftover fabrics.

The front is a mix of navy florals, black-and-white stripes, denim, cobalt blue, a blue-and-white graphic print and a shimmering iridescent scrap, framed by black perforated fabric on the sides and base. Turn it around and the back is a calm deep blue polka-dot fabric.

Three different straps (black, striped and chambray blue) make it comfortable on the shoulder and unmistakably yours.

Pieces aren't sold online. Curious about this one? Send me a message and I'll tell you more.
