Reversible Tropical Patchwork Tote Bag

Palm Leaf and Parrot Print

ReversiblePockets

Jungle on one side, graphic on the other, cut and sewn by hand.

One side is a patchwork of tropical prints pieced into big diamond shapes: palm leaves, monstera, a parrot, hibiscus and bird of paradise flowers. Turn it around for a black and grey geometric print with four outer pockets for your phone, keys and sunglasses. Flip it back and the pockets are on the inside.

Pieces aren't sold online. Curious about this one? Send me a message and I'll tell you more.
